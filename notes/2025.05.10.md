Now I need to think of what exactly to do next. As I mentioned, odometry sort of works. SLAM: meh not exactly sure, but seems to fine as well. I can continue improving it, or I can try integrating with what rovertech actually wants:
control connected RP Pico via UART using Mavlink protocol, and fuse with GPS data.

Problems:
1. Their Pico firmware is designed for their platform, and I would need to reconfigure it to my board. Not a big deal regarding motors, I guess its just a question of setting up correct pins, and maybe figuring out that there are 4 drives instead of 2.
2. Fusing with GPS and control. Two options:
    a. Control over RT controller: I will receive GPS data from RT system, fuse it with VO and IMU in robot localization, then send odom back to RT controller. RT controller will be responsible for interaction with qGroundControl, we just going to make it a little bit more aware of its surroundings.
    Pros: fits nice into existing and tested architecture. I don't need to integrate with qGroundControl on RPi.
    Cons: Little options for more sophisticated planning with ROS, like obstacles detection, rerouting, etc.
    b. Control over ROS: I will receive GPS either from RT or directly in RP, fuse it with VO and IMU, implement interaction with qGroundControl, route planning and motors control in ROS.
    Pros: a lot of additional things we can do with image recognition, obstacle detection, and everything else ROS has to offer.
    Cons: less control over ROS as a distributed system, no one is an expert in it, and folks know the stack of their RT from top to bottom.

I think I should go with A for now, and prove that this will allow a logistic drone to follow a missing in case of
GPS service decline. But I do want to implement B, and prove that its a viable option. I need to discuss this with ChatGPT and the boys.

My own research: option A is traditional architecture for UAV because they require most of their stack to be on RT flight controller. And ArduPilot can do a lot of things quite fast, like sensor fusion etc. It can't do VO, but it
can receive VO over mavlink. Example: https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/ros-vio-tracking-camera.html

Something similar to option B described here https://docs.px4.io/main/en/ros/offboard_control.html.
